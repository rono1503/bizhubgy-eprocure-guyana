Frequently Asked Questions
What is e-Procure Guyana? expand_more
e-Procure (eprocure.gov.gy) is Guyana's official electronic government procurement portal. All government ministries and agencies publish tender opportunities there.
Do I need an e-Procure account? expand_more
Yes, you need an account to submit bids. BizHubGY helps you create and manage your profile, and we monitor the portal on your behalf.
Is e-Procure free to use? expand_more
Yes, accessing e-Procure is free. BizHubGY's monitoring and alert service starts at $0 on the Free plan (10 alerts/month).
How many agencies use e-Procure? expand_more
31+ agencies including Office of the President, Ministry of Public Works, Ministry of Health, GRA, NIS, and all regional democratic councils.
What if I miss a tender that isn't on e-Procure? expand_more
We also monitor NPTA publications, agency-specific bulletins, and News Room announcements for opportunities not posted to e-Procure.
